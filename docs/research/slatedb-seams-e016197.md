## Not observed in the inspected revision

- No public `snapshot_at(sequence)` or equivalent explicit read-version entrypoint.
- No public standalone SST builder or table-store publication API.
- No distributed resolver, global read-version authority, or cross-process
  strict-serializability protocol.
- No per-range manifest or serving-owner generation. SlateDB segments remain
  subordinate to one database manifest and WAL.
- No published `slatedb-dst` crate or stable external DST compatibility promise.

These are source-absence observations for the pinned revision, not claims that
the capabilities cannot be implemented.
