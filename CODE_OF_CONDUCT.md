# Code of conduct

We want objectKV collaboration to be precise, open, and safe for disagreement.

- Challenge ideas and evidence, not people.
- State uncertainty and tradeoffs directly.
- Keep review focused on the invariant and observed behavior.
- Do not harass, discriminate against, threaten, or expose private information
  about another participant.
- Respect maintainer decisions on project scope and safety.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository owners. Maintainers may
remove content or restrict participation when needed to protect contributors and
the project.
